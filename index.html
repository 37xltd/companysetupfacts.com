How it works

A dependable answer starts with a dependable join.

We prioritise the exact company number, then attach public facts to that permanent identity. Names and aliases are useful search keys, but they do not replace identity resolution.

We show source, observation date, match confidence and coverage on the page. Calculated signals are labelled as calculated; unknown, not published and not checked are kept distinct.
